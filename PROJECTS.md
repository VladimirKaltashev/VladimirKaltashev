# Project notes

The source repositories for these projects are private.

## Build Your Dream Castle

A browser-based 3D sandbox where children can place and stack towers, draw walls, and erase individual sections. The camera supports mouse and touch controls. The prototype uses Three.js, TypeScript, and Vite.

## Doonext

A Telegram Mini App I built to plan my tasks. It supports deadlines, priorities, and subtasks, and shows completed work on a calendar heatmap. It uses React, TypeScript, and Cloudflare D1.

## SAT Practice Trainer

A local app for timed SAT Reading and Writing practice. I can pause a session, review my answers, and compare how much time I spend per question. It supports JSON imports and can extract questions from PDFs locally on macOS using PDFKit.
